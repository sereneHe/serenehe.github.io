abstract: Exact score-based causal structure learning supports explicit structural constraints and optimisation certificates, but its branch-and-cut search can be computationally demanding. We augment exact causal structure learning with an upper bound on the maximum clique size of the learned skeleton, enforced at integer candidates through lazy cycle and clique constraints, and study callback order, clique-cut policies, incumbent repair, fractional separation and Big-M bounds on Erdős–Rényi and scale-free DAGs. Tighter valid Big-M bounds reduce the fixed-budget optimality gap in most settings, while the benefit of clique constraints depends on graph density, sample size and cut management.
